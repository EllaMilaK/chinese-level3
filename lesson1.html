🚦 红绿灯的故事 · About Traffic Lights

红绿灯是城市交通的好帮手！世界上第一个红绿灯出现在1868年的英国伦敦。


红灯表示停止，绿灯表示前行，黄灯表示注意。


小朋友过马路时，要手拉手，看红绿灯，保证安全！

Traffic lights are a city's best helper! The world's first traffic light appeared in London, England in 1868.


Red means stop, green means go, and yellow means caution.


Children should hold hands when crossing the street and always follow the traffic lights to stay safe!

🌍 趣味知识： 全世界超过100个国家都用红绿灯！Fun fact: Over 100 countries around the world use traffic lights!
