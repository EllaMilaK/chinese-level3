📚 书店里的书 · Books in the Bookstore

书店里有各种各样的书。童话故事让孩子们进入奇妙的世界，儿童画报用图画讲故事。


著名的童话作家有安徒生（丹麦），他写了《小美人鱼》《卖火柴的小女孩》。


中国也有很多著名的儿童故事，比如《西游记》里的孙悟空！

Bookstores have all kinds of books. Fairy tales take children into magical worlds, while picture magazines tell stories through illustrations.


Famous fairy tale author Hans Christian Andersen (Denmark) wrote The Little Mermaid and The Little Match Girl.


China also has famous children's stories, like the Monkey King from Journey to the West!

📖 趣味知识： 中国每年出版超过50万种图书！Fun fact: China publishes over 500,000 different books every year!
