🏥 中国医院文化 · Hospital Culture in China

中国的医院分为门诊（看病）和住院两部分。病人去医院时，先要挂号，然后等医生叫到自己的名字才能进去看病。


护士负责量体温、打针、发药。医生负责检查身体、开药方。中国小朋友生病时，爸爸妈妈都会陪他们去医院。

Chinese hospitals have outpatient clinics (门诊) and inpatient wards. When visiting, patients first register (挂号) before seeing a doctor.


Nurses take temperatures, give injections, and hand out medicine. Doctors examine patients and write prescriptions. Parents always accompany children to the hospital.

💊 常用医疗词语： 量体温 (take temperature) · 打针 (injection) · 吃药 (take medicine) · 放心 (feel relieved) · 检查 (check-up)
